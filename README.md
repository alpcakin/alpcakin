### Projects

- **[lore](https://github.com/alpcakin/lore)**: a command palette for your shell. Press `ctrl+g`, type a few words of what you're trying to do, and the finished command lands on your prompt. Written in Rust, published on crates.io as [`cmdlore`](https://crates.io/crates/cmdlore).
- **[plainfold](https://github.com/alpcakin/plainfold)**: reads a codebase and shows what it does as sequence diagrams, with every step leading down to the real lines. Rust, tree-sitter, language servers, Tauri.
- **[odesh](https://odesh.app)**: tracks shared debts and expenses between friends and groups, live on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/tr/app/odesh/id6802964428). I designed the PostgreSQL schema, the settlement logic and the Supabase backend.
- **[TradeArena](https://github.com/alpcakin/TradeArena)**: a realtime multiplayer stock trading game built from C#/.NET microservices with RabbitMQ and SignalR.
- **[AI Financial Insights System](https://github.com/alpcakin/ai-financial-insights-system)**: my bachelor's thesis. A FastAPI backend that ingests market data and sends AI insights and alerts to a Flutter app.

I also write about the things I am curious about on [Substack](https://substack.com/@alpcakin) and my own website.
